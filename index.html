How my main platform works

A multi-tenant financial document platform for construction companies. Invoices arrive by email, upload or scan, get read by OCR, pass validation and duplicate checks, and post to each client’s ERP through an idempotent queue.

How I work

What I believe about building software

Correctness comes first

In financial systems a duplicate or lost record is real money. I design for retries, idempotency and reconciliation from the start, not after the first incident.

One model, many systems

Integrations stay maintainable when the core speaks one language and every external system gets its own adapter. That’s how one platform talks to six ERPs.

Own it end to end

From understanding a client’s workflow to architecture, deployment and support. Tests and validation are part of the work, not something added at the end.
